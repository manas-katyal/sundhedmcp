How SundhedMCP works

sundhed.dk has no API for citizens. So SundhedMCP does what your browser does: you log in with MitID, and it reads the same pages you see on Min Side.

1 
~ claude mcp add sundhedmcp \
    -- npx -y sundhedmcp
✓ Added stdio MCP server sundhedmcp

~ claude
› connect sundhed.dk

Add it to your AI

One command in Claude Code, or a custom connector in claude.ai when you host it on Railway. Works with any MCP client.

2

Log in with MitID

The real MitID login opens, ready for your user ID. Type it, then approve in your MitID app like any other login.

3

Ask anything

Your AI picks the right tool and answers from your own record, in plain words and in your language.

Privacy

Private by design.

Your health record is yours. SundhedMCP is built so it stays that way.

You approve every login.

You log in on the real MitID page and approve in your own MitID app. There is no password to hand over, and nothing happens until you approve.

Nothing is stored.

The login lives in memory. Stop SundhedMCP and it is gone. No database, no logs of your data, no telemetry.

Read-only.

It can read your record. It cannot renew a prescription, change your medicine card or send a message.

CPR stays out.

Your CPR number is masked in every answer before your AI sees it.

Runs where you choose.

On your own computer, or on a server you control. There is no SundhedMCP company in between.

Open source.

MIT licensed and small enough to read in an afternoon. Check it yourself.

Get started

Two ways to run it.

Both read the same record with the same tools. Pick where the browser that holds your login should live.

On your computer

Local.

For Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Cursor. A browser window opens for MitID and minimizes itself.

  • Your data never leaves your machine, except to your AI
  • Needs Node 24 and Chrome or Edge
claude mcp add sundhedmcp -- npx -y sundhedmcp

On your own server

Hosted.

For claude.ai and your phone. Deploy to Railway in a few minutes. You log in with MitID on a password-protected page on your own server.

  1. Deploy to Railway. With the Railway CLI, from a copy of the code: 
    git clone https://github.com/manas-katyal/sundhedmcp
cd sundhedmcp
railway init --name sundhedmcp
railway add --service sundhedmcp \
  --variables "ADMIN_PASSWORD=$(openssl rand -base64 24)"
railway service link sundhedmcp
railway volume add --mount-path /data
railway domain
railway up
  2. Add it to claude.ai. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, with your Railway address plus /mcp. Sign in with the password.
  3. Log in with MitID. Ask Claude anything about your health. It sends you a link to your server, where you type your MitID user ID and approve in the app.

Any Docker host works too: the image needs ADMIN_PASSWORD and a volume at /data. Your data passes through your server's memory and is never stored.

In development: the npm package is not published yet, so run it locally from source. Railway builds from source already.
