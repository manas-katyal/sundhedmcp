Your AI now reads your health record.

SundhedMCP connects your sundhed.dk data to Claude or any MCP client: your medicine card, prescriptions, test results, vaccinations and referrals. You log in with MitID yourself. It is read-only and runs on your own machine.

Get started

Three steps, about five minutes.

No developer account and no API key. sundhed.dk has no public API for citizens, so SundhedMCP uses your own logged-in session, the same way your browser does.

Step 1

Add it to your AI

Needs Node 24 or newer. Works with any client that runs a local MCP server.

claude mcp add sundhedmcp \
  -- npx -y sundhedmcp

Step 2

Log in with MitID

Say "connect sundhed.dk". A browser window opens on sundhed.dk. Approve in the MitID app and the window hides itself.

Step 3

Ask anything

Your AI calls the tools it needs. When sundhed.dk ends the session, it tells you and you log in again.

What it reads

Everything you can see on Min Side, nothing you cannot.

Each area becomes a read-only tool. It uses the same pages sundhed.dk shows you after you log in, so it sees what you see.

Medicine card Medicinkort

Current and stopped medicine: drug, strength, dosage, reason, ATC code, who prescribed it and when.

Prescriptions Recepter

Open and closed prescriptions, dispensings left, expiry dates, pharmacy and package size.

Test results Prøvesvar

Blood and lab results from regional labs for any date range, with reference intervals.

Vaccinations Vaccinationer

Every registered vaccination, what it protects against, how long it covers and who gave it.

Referrals Henvisninger

Active and earlier referrals to specialists and hospitals.

Things to ask

Your own record, in plain words.

  • Which prescriptions do I need to renew before I travel in December?
  • How has my cholesterol changed across my last three blood tests?
  • What do I need to get vaccinated against for a trip to Vietnam?
  • List everything I take, with doses, so I can show a new doctor.
  • Do any of my medicines contain the same active substance?
  • Has my referral to the dermatologist been received yet?

Security

Your health record stays with you.

Runs on your machine

No server, no account, no telemetry. The only place your data goes is the AI client you ask.

You log in, not the software

MitID happens on sundhed.dk in a real browser window. SundhedMCP never sees a password or your MitID app.

Session lives in memory

The login is held by the browser while SundhedMCP runs. Stop it and the session is gone. Nothing is written to disk.

Read-only

No renewals, no changes to your medicine card, no messages. It can only read what is already there.

CPR stays out of answers

Your CPR number is removed from every tool result before your AI sees it.

Open source

MIT licensed and small enough to read in an afternoon, so you can check all of the above yourself.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this made by sundhed.dk?

No. SundhedMCP is an independent open-source project, not affiliated with sundhed.dk, Sundhedsdatastyrelsen or MitID.

Why do I have to log in again?

sundhed.dk ends sessions after a while, and SundhedMCP does not keep your login between restarts. When a tool says the session has ended, say "connect sundhed.dk" again.

Can I run it for my family or my patients?

No. It is for one person reading their own record on their own machine. A service that holds other people's health data needs approval, agreements and certificates it does not have.

Does it work in claude.ai or on my phone?

Not yet. It needs a browser window for MitID on the same machine, so it runs with desktop clients like Claude Code and Claude Desktop.

What happens if sundhed.dk changes its site?

A tool may stop working until SundhedMCP is updated. It fails with a clear message rather than guessing.

What it is not

Not medical advice. Your AI can read and summarise your record, but it is not your doctor. Talk to your GP or pharmacist before changing how you take any medicine.

Not an official integration. The official Fælles Medicinkort API is for approved healthcare systems. SundhedMCP reads the citizen pages you already have access to.
