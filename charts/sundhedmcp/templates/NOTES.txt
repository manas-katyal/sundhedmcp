Open {{ .Values.baseUrl }} on a computer.

{{- if include "sundhedmcp.multiUser" . }}

Several people, signed in with {{ .Values.oidc.issuer }}. Each person logs in
to sundhed.dk with MitID at {{ .Values.baseUrl }}/connect, after signing in
there with their own account (on a computer: MitID shows a QR code for the app
on their phone). Their /mcp calls need an access token from the same issuer,
with "{{ .Values.oidc.audience }}" in its audience:

  {{ .Values.baseUrl }}/mcp
{{- else }}

Log in to sundhed.dk with MitID at {{ .Values.baseUrl }}/connect (on a computer: MitID
shows a QR code there for the app on your phone). The server password:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "sundhedmcp.passwordSecret" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.{{ .Values.adminPassword.secretKey }}}' | base64 -d; echo

MCP endpoint for clients that sign in with OAuth (claude.ai, Claude Desktop):

  {{ .Values.baseUrl }}/mcp
{{- end }}
